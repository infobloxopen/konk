etcd can be accessed via port {{ .Values.service.port }} on the following DNS name from within your cluster:

    {{ include "etcd.fullname" . }}.{{ .Release.Namespace }}.svc.{{ .Values.clusterDomain }}

To connect to your etcd server from outside the cluster:

    kubectl port-forward --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} svc/{{ include "etcd.fullname" . }} {{ .Values.service.port }}:{{ .Values.service.port }}

Then you can connect using:

    etcdctl --endpoints=http://127.0.0.1:{{ .Values.service.port }} endpoint health
